About

From the shop floor to the CEO's office

I've spent the last eight years inside one of India's largest aluminium businesses, moving between strategy, sales, operations, and supply chain — from developing growth frameworks in the CEO's office to negotiating contracts directly with customers on the ground. That range is deliberate: strategy that hasn't been tested against a production line or a customer conversation tends to stay theoretical.

I'm most useful in rooms where a strategic idea has to survive contact with execution.
